Who we are

Built by people who've sat in the waiting room too.

PulseCare started with a front desk that was still calling out token numbers by hand and a doctor's queue that lived on a whiteboard. We built the system we wished that clinic had — one place where check-in, the live queue, the consultation, and the record all agree with each other.

We work directly with clinic staff, not just IT departments, because the person managing a queue on a busy Monday morning is the one who knows what the software actually needs to do.
